GUIDE 01 / BEFORE YOU FILM

Start with one clear viewer question.

Define who the video helps, what it answers, and what makes your explanation worth watching.

Open the planning checklist

Write the viewer’s question in one sentence. Sketch the opening. Choose a relevant phrase naturally. Plan examples that actually support your title.

GUIDE 02 / PACKAGE THE STORY

A title and thumbnail that work together.

Give viewers an understandable reason to click without promising something the video cannot deliver.

Open the packaging checklist

Make the subject clear. Keep thumbnail text readable at small sizes. Avoid repeating the full title on the image. Check that both accurately represent the video.

GUIDE 03 / AFTER YOU UPLOAD

Learn from the viewing experience.

Review your own channel data in context and use it to form a testable idea for your next upload.

Open the review checklist

Compare similar formats and time windows. Note confusing moments, early exits, and useful comments. Change one creative variable at a time when practical.
